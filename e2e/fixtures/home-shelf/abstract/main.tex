\begin{center}
  {\Large\bfseries Mapping Soil Moisture with Cosmic-Ray Neutrons}\\[1em]
  P.~Varga, S.~Nakamura\\[0.3em]
  \emph{Extended abstract}
\end{center}

\bigskip
Cosmic-ray neutron sensors estimate soil moisture over an area of several
hectares, far larger than point probes cover. We report a season of
measurements from a mixed farm and compare them with a grid of buried probes.

The sensor tracked the probe average closely during dry spells and lagged by
about a day after heavy rain. We discuss corrections for vegetation water and
the placement of a second sensor.
