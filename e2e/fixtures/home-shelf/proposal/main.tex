\section{Summary}
Tidal flats change faster than satellite surveys can follow. We propose a
network of buoy-mounted sensors that record water level, turbidity and
temperature every ten minutes, at a tenth of the cost of current stations.

\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Build and field-test twenty sensor buoys.
  \item Publish an open data set covering two full seasons.
  \item Compare the measurements with existing survey stations.
\end{enumerate}
