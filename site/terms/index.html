Terms of use for the hosted beta

These terms cover the closed beta of hosted Aldine at app.aldine.dev.

Version of 21 November 2026.

1. Who runs this

The hosted beta of Aldine at app.aldine.dev ("the service") is run by Tobias Rahloff, Dannenkamp 6, 22869 Schenefeld, Germany, hello@aldine.dev ("we"). Aldine itself is open-source software (AGPL-3.0); these terms cover the hosted service, not the software.

2. What the beta is

  1. A closed beta: access is by invitation only. We approve each account.
  2. It runs for six months from the day your account is approved ("beta period"). Section 9 says what happens after.
  3. It is free. We will not charge beta groups for anything during the beta period, and we will tell you at least three months ahead if anything you use would stop being free afterwards.
  4. It is a beta: features change, things break, and we may restart or update the service at short notice, usually with each Aldine release.

3. What we ask of you

  1. Tell us when something is slow, broken or missing (GitHub issues or hello@aldine.dev). That is the point of the beta.
  2. Keep your sign-in secure, and do not share accounts. Invite co-authors or create share links instead.
  3. Use the service for research and teaching writing, within the law. In particular, do not upload content you have no right to, malware, or material that infringes others' rights, and do not try to break the service, other users' projects or the typesetting sandbox.
  4. Respect the limits we set (typesetting time, storage) and do not work around them.

4. Your content

  1. Everything you put into the service stays yours. We claim no rights to it.
  2. You allow us to store, copy, typeset and display it, only as far as the service needs to work for you and the people you share it with, and to back it up.
  3. We do not read your projects. We look at a project only when you ask us to (support), when the law requires it, or to stop a concrete attack or misuse, and we tell you afterwards where the law allows.
  4. We do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not sell or pass it on. No AI features run on this instance unless you connect one yourself (for example through the Agent API), and then that tool is yours.

5. Sharing

You decide who sees a project: co-authors you invite, and share links you create (with a role and, if you like, an expiry). Anyone with a share link can open the project with that link's role. Revoke a link when it is no longer needed. People who join through your link are your responsibility toward us, the same as co-authors you invite.

6. Availability and backups

  1. We aim to keep the service running but promise no uptime during the beta.
  2. We take a full backup every night and keep backups for about 35 days, stored in the EU. Backups protect against our mistakes, not yours: we cannot restore single files on request during the beta.
  3. Keep your own copy of anything important. Every project can be downloaded as a ZIP at any time, and synced to GitHub or GitLab.

7. Personal data

  1. The privacy policy says which personal data we process, why, where, and for how long.
  2. For the account data of each user we are the controller.
  3. A group that keeps other people's personal data in its projects (for example a research group storing manuscripts with co-authors' data) can sign our data processing agreement with us (Art. 28 GDPR).

8. Liability

  1. The service is free. We are liable without limit for intent and gross negligence, for injury to life, body or health, and under the Product Liability Act.
  2. Beyond that we are not liable, in particular not for slight negligence, for data loss that a regular copy of your own would have prevented, or for interruptions.

9. Ending

  1. You can stop at any time: delete your projects and your account, or ask us to.
  2. We can end the beta, or your access, with at least three months' notice by email. Within that time you can download all your projects. We delete remaining data 30 days after the end, and backups roll off within 35 more days.
  3. We can block access immediately if you seriously break section 3, or to stop an attack. We tell you why.
  4. After the beta period we offer to move your account to whatever follows (a self-service offer with a free tier is planned). Nothing moves to a paid plan without your explicit choice.

10. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms, and tell you by email at least six weeks ahead. If you do not agree, you can stop using the service; section 9.1 applies.
