Privacy policy

This policy covers the hosted beta of Aldine at app.aldine.dev. It says which personal data we process, why, where and for how long.

Last updated: 21 November 2026. The terms of use for the beta are at aldine.dev/terms/.

1. Who is responsible

Tobias Rahloff, Dannenkamp 6, 22869 Schenefeld, Germany, hello@aldine.dev ("we").

For content a research group keeps in its projects, the group's institution may be the controller and we its processor under a data processing agreement. In that case the group is your first contact for that content.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The service sets three cookies. All three are needed for it to work, so no consent is asked for:

  • aldine_session keeps you signed in, for 30 days.
  • aldine_device remembers that this browser signed in before, so failed sign-in attempts from elsewhere cannot lock you out, for 365 days.
  • aldine_guest keeps a guest's access through a share link, for 30 days.

Your browser's local storage holds display settings (theme, editor preferences). They never leave your browser.

4. Legal bases

  • Running the service for you: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the beta terms).
  • Security, rate limits and error reports: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest in a secure, working service.
  • Data of third parties inside your documents: whoever puts it there is responsible for having a legal basis. For a research group, see section 1.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Much of this you can do yourself: account settings let you download all your data and delete your account. For anything else, write to hello@aldine.dev.

You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for us: Unabhängiges Landeszentrum für Datenschutz Schleswig-Holstein (ULD), Kiel.

7. Changes

We update this policy when the service changes, and tell account holders by email about changes that matter.
