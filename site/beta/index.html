Hosted Aldine for research groups

Aldine runs at app.aldine.dev for research groups that would rather not run a server. The beta is invitation-only, free for six months, and needs no credit card.

Request access Try the demo first

The demo needs no account. It is a shared sandbox and resets every night.

What you get

The same Aldine you could install yourself, with nothing held back:

  • A collaborative LaTeX editor: co-authors edit at the same time and see each other's cursors, and typesetting runs on the server, so nobody installs TeX.
  • Visual mode, for co-authors who would rather not read LaTeX source.
  • Git history for every project, and sync with GitHub or GitLab.
  • Zotero: link your library or a group library and cite from it.
  • Share links, including for guests who have no account.
  • Comments and suggested changes the author accepts with one click.
  • The Agent API, so Claude and other assistants can read, edit and typeset a project you connect them to. The LaTeX MCP server guide shows how.

What we ask in return

Tell us when something is slow, broken or missing. Write to hello@aldine.dev or open a GitHub issue. That feedback is what the beta is for.

Your data

  • The servers are in Germany, at Hetzner. Backups run every night, are kept in the EU, and a restore is tested every month.
  • Your data stays yours. We do not read your projects, do not train AI models on them, and do not sell or pass them on.
  • You can download everything as a ZIP at any time, and delete your account yourself in account settings.
  • Institutions can sign a data processing agreement with us. Ask at hello@aldine.dev.

The details are in the privacy policy, the terms of use and the legal notice.

How to join

Request access with the button below. The sign-up form asks what your group will use Aldine for. An admin reads that and approves the account, and you get an email when it is ready.

After the beta

A free tier stays. Nothing moves to a paid plan unless you choose it. If anything you use would stop being free, or the beta ends, you hear from us by email at least three months ahead, with time to download your projects.

Prefer to run it yourself?

Aldine is open source (AGPL-3.0) and runs as two Docker containers. The self-hosting guide covers the install, upgrades, sign-in and backups.

ALDINE, after the Aldine Press of Aldus Manutius, Venice 1494, where scholarly publishing became portable. Festina lente.
